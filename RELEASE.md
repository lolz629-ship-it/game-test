## The Little Wild
A standalone Windows desktop game. Download **VerdantEngine.zip**, extract the complete folder, and open **Verdant Engine.exe**.

- Six animated cartoon companions with different board interactions.
- Sixteen workshop blueprints with new abilities at evolution levels 3 and 6.
- Completely rebuilt prestige: choose a free keepsake after a full restoration, discover sixteen board-changing powers, and equip three for each expedition.
- Portals, mushroom trampolines, hatchable eggs, crew picnics, lightning webs, flower chains, and friendly meteors.
- 240 Hz simulation, hardware acceleration, and selectable frame targets up to 240 FPS. Actual FPS depends on your monitor and hardware; a live counter shows performance.
- Clearer ability, character, and upgrade descriptions. Original ethereal soundtrack with separate music/effect controls.
- Shorter expeditions: tested starter simulations finish in approximately 101–159 seconds with frequent purchases.

**Keep your saves:** put the new extracted game folder beside your existing **VerdantEngine-saves** folder. Version 1 and 2 saves migrate automatically; old root-network purchases are refunded in full as seeds. Original save files remain intact. You can also export/import backups in Settings. Version 3 saves cannot be opened in older versions.

Validated with 16 automated gameplay/migration tests and a Windows desktop launch, save, workshop, music, and settings smoke test.
